PRIVACY

WERLD/ is designed to know as little about you as possible.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no accounts.

What stays in your browser

To show what's new since your last visit, WERLD/ keeps a few small values in your own browser's local storage: the time of your last visit, how many reports a story had when you last opened it, and whether you turned the screen effect off. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Other services your browser talks to

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives standard web request data such as your IP address in order to serve pages.
  • The map outlines and map code are loaded from jsDelivr.
  • Video pages can play WERLD/ videos from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. Those services set their own cookies and trackers, so the player only loads when you choose to play it.
  • Links to news outlets take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

People in the news

WERLD/ links to and quotes headlines from news reporting, which can name people. We do this for journalism in the public interest. If you are named in a story on WERLD/ and want it reviewed or removed, email binning.ai.incorporated@gmail.com with the story code. We will consider every request, weighing your rights against the public interest, and tell you what we decide and why.

If you contact us

We use your email only to deal with your message, keep it no longer than needed for that (and any related correction record), and never share it or add you to a mailing list.

Who is responsible

Binning.AI, United Kingdom: binning.ai.incorporated@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
