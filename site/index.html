    1. 01
      The outlet's words, not ours.Headlines are shown as each outlet published them. No model rewrites or summarises them on this page.
      2. 
        
    2. 02
      Every story has a source you can check.Each line links to the original reporting, with every outlet that carried it.
      3. 
        
    3. 03
      Counting origins, not copies.When several outlets report the same event, WERLD/ merges them into one story. Outlets running near-identical wording (usually one wire report, syndicated) count as one origin. A story is CONFIRMED only with two or more independent origins; otherwise it is marked SINGLE SOURCE.
      4. 
        
    4. 04
      Your clock, not ours.Times are shown in your own time zone. "NEW" marks stories that arrived since your last visit.
      5. 
        
    5. 05
      If the engine stops, the page says so.The status light turns amber and shows when the feed last updated, instead of pretending it's live.
      6. 
        
    6. 06
      A draft, and corrections are welcome.WERLD is in development. Place tags and desk labels are assigned automatically and can be wrong. Spot one? Report it.
      7. 
        
    7. 07
      No tracking.No cookies, no analytics, no third-party fonts. The map outline loads from jsDelivr.
