    HOW WERLD WORKS

    1. 01
      The outlet's words, not ours.Headlines are shown as each outlet published them. No model rewrites or summarises them on this page.
      2. 
        
    2. 02
      Every story has a source you can check.Each line links to the original reporting, with every outlet that carried it.
      3. 
        
    3. 03
      Counting origins, not copies.When several outlets report the same event, WERLD/ merges them into one story. Outlets running near-identical wording (usually one wire report, syndicated) count as one origin. A story is marked MULTI-SOURCE when two or more independent origins report it, otherwise SINGLE SOURCE. Neither label means WERLD/ has verified the facts: it tells you how many separate outlets are reporting them.
      4. 
        
    4. 04
      Your clock, not ours.Times are shown in your own time zone. "NEW" marks stories that arrived since your last visit.
      5. 
        
    5. 05
      If the engine stops, the page says so.The status light turns amber and shows when the feed last updated, instead of pretending it's live.
      6. 
        
    6. 06
      A draft, and corrections are welcome.WERLD is in development. Place tags and desk labels are assigned automatically and can be wrong. Spot one? Email us, and see our editorial standards.
      7. 
        
    7. 07
      English-language reporting, for now.We only publish what we can read and check. Reporting in other languages is filtered out until we can translate it properly.
      8. 
        
    8. 08
      Sensitive stories are handled with care.Stories involving sexual offences, suicide, harm to children or live UK legal proceedings are held back and only appear after a human editor has checked them. Even then they are never featured in the Brief, the ticker or a video. Sanctioned state outlets (RT, Sputnik, RIA) are not used, and other state-funded outlets are labelled. Sport and entertainment are outside our beat.
      9. 
        
    9. 09
      No tracking.No cookies, no analytics, no third-party fonts. The map outline loads from jsDelivr.
