ABOUT WERLD/

WERLD/ is the live intelligence engine from Binning.AI. It shows what is happening in the world right now, who is reporting it, and where, with every story linked to the original reporting.

Who publishes WERLD/

WERLD/ is published by Binning.AI, an independent technology venture based in the United Kingdom. Binning.AI is responsible for what appears on WERLD/ and for the WERLD/ video briefs on TikTok and Instagram.

WERLD/ is independent. It has no owner other than Binning.AI, takes no money from governments, political parties or the outlets it links to, and carries no advertising.

Contact

Corrections, complaints, takedown requests and everything else: binning.ai.incorporated@gmail.com. Please include the story code (five characters, shown on every story page) if you have one. We aim to reply within 24 hours. See the complaints and takedown policy.

What WERLD/ is, and is not

WERLD/ is a wire: it gathers reporting from around 200 news organisations, agencies, official sources and hazard alerts, groups reports of the same event together, and shows how many independent outlets are covering it. Each headline is the outlet's own, credited and linked. WERLD/ does not rewrite headlines, write articles, or claim to have verified the facts itself.

Labels such as MULTI-SOURCE, the desks, places and threads, and the order of the Brief are produced by software, following published rules (see Editorial standards). Software makes mistakes; if you spot one, tell us.

Where it comes from

WERLD/ is built on Binning.AI's Intel Engine, which collects public news feeds and alerts around the clock. Hazard alerts come from GDACS (European Commission JRC and UN OCHA). Map outlines are from Natural Earth. Fonts are Bebas Neue and IBM Plex Mono, under the SIL Open Font License.
